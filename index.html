All Brick & Flint Work

  • ✓ Authentic knapped and field flint matching
  • ✓ Breathable non-hydraulic & hydraulic lime mortar

Woodwork & Carpentry

Structural roof trusses, traditional oak timber framing, studwork, bespoke staircases, architectural skirtings, sash window restorations, and solid hardwood doors.

  • ✓ 1st & 2nd fix architectural carpentry
  • ✓ Green oak & seasoned timber restoration
  • ✓ Custom mouldings & period architraves

Complete Roofing

Norfolk red pantile roofs, natural slate, breathable underlay membranes, chimney stack re-pointing, lead valleys, flashings, and high-performance flat roof systems.

  • ✓ Full strip and re-tile with heritage pantiles
  • ✓ Code 4 & 5 milled lead dressed valleys

Drainage & Groundworks

Sub-surface rainwater soakaways, foul sewer connections, trenching, French drains around damp flint footings, inspection chambers, and footings.

  • ✓ Resolving water ingress around period walls
  • ✓ Building regulation compliant storm drains
  • ✓ Deep trenching & concrete strip foundations
